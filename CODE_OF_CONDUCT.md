# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This project exists to make documents usable by people who rely on assistive
technology. Holding ourselves to a welcoming standard is part of the same work.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps the project:

- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use what we build
- Showing empathy towards other contributors

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions and code review, and when an individual is representing the project
in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the project maintainers by opening a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/krishaygarg/ada_pdf_remediation/security/advisories/new),
which is not publicly visible, or by contacting a maintainer directly. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers
are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not align with this code of conduct, and will explain
their reasoning when doing so.
