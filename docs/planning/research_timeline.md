# Research Roadmap & Timeline (3-Month Plan)

This document outlines a high-level timeline and roadmap for the research project over a 10-to-12-week period (approximately 2.5 to 3 months). The roadmap represents the general stages required to design, implement, evaluate, and publish the research.

---

## High-Level Phase Breakdown

*   **Phase 1 (Weeks 1-2):** Literature Review & Setup
*   **Phase 2 (Weeks 3-4):** Baseline Implementation & Analysis
*   **Phase 3 (Weeks 5-7):** Core Methodology & Tuning
*   **Phase 4 (Weeks 8-9):** System Integration & Evaluation
*   **Phase 5 (Weeks 10-12):** Paper Drafting & Submission

## Detailed Weekly Roadmap

### Phase 1: Literature Review & Setup (Weeks 1-2)
Establish the theoretical foundation and prepare the experimental infrastructure.
*   **Literature Survey:** Review recent publications, identify gaps in prior work, and solidify research questions.
*   **Evaluation Protocol:** Select and define quantitative and qualitative metrics for measuring system performance.
*   **Data Preparation:** Assemble representative benchmark datasets (train, validation, and test splits).
*   **Environment Configuration:** Setup the code repository, toolchains, and environment dependencies.

### Phase 2: Baseline Implementation & Analysis (Weeks 3-4)
Implement baseline solutions to establish reference points for comparison.
*   **Baseline Deployment:** Run standard/existing solutions on the benchmark datasets.
*   **Resource & Quality Profiling:** Measure key baseline attributes such as accuracy, latency, and resource costs.
*   **Error Analysis:** Perform detailed analysis of baseline failure modes to guide the design of the proposed method.

### Phase 3: Core Methodology & Tuning (Weeks 5-7)
Design, build, and optimize the primary research contribution.
*   **Algorithm Prototyping:** Implement the proposed system architecture and core pipeline components.
*   **Iterative Tuning:** Adjust hyper-parameters, configurations, and internal pipeline stages to optimize performance.
*   **Ablation Studies:** Systematically disable individual system components to understand their impact.

### Phase 4: System Integration & Evaluation (Weeks 8-9)
Validate the completed system against established baselines and constraints.
*   **System Integration:** Integrate any modular components into a single, cohesive runtime pipeline.
*   **Comparative Benchmark:** Run comparative experiments measuring the proposed method against baseline configurations.
*   **Efficiency Analysis:** Conduct trade-off analysis between solution quality, compute/financial costs, and latency.

### Phase 5: Paper Drafting & Submission (Weeks 10-12)
Compile findings and draft the research manuscript for peer review.
*   **Drafting the Manuscript:** Write the introduction, related work, methodology, experiments, and discussion sections.
*   **Data Visualizations:** Generate plots, charts, and tables to effectively present the experimental findings.
*   **Review & Refinement:** Conduct internal reviews and copy-editing to polish the paper.
*   **Submission Preparation:** Format the document in accordance with the target workshop guidelines and submit.
